## How Microsoft certifications work

- [Browse all credentials](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/browse/) - Official catalog with filters by role, product and level.
- [Exam scoring and score reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/exam-scoring-reports) - Scaled score, 700 out of 1000 to pass on every exam.
- [Exam duration and experience](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/exam-duration-exam-experience) - Question types, interactive components and time per exam.
- [Retake policy](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/retake-policy) - Retake 24 hours after the first fail; longer waits after that.
- [Renew your certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/renew-your-microsoft-certification) - Associate, expert and specialty certifications expire yearly; renewal is a free online assessment.
- [Exam sandbox](https://aka.ms/examdemo) - Try the real exam interface and question types before exam day.
- [Request accommodations](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/request-accommodations) - Extra time, assistive devices, language accommodations.
- [Schedule through Pearson VUE](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/schedule-through-pearson-vue) - Booking, prices by country, online proctored or test center.

## Exam changes and retirements (2026)

| Exam | Change | Date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PL-400 | Transitions to **AB-400** (same certification, updated skills outline with agents, Copilot Studio, Foundry, code apps). AB-400 bookable now. | AB-400 available from 16 October 2026 |
| MS-102 | Exam and **Microsoft 365 Certified: Administrator Expert** retire; cannot be earned or renewed afterwards. | 30 November 2026 |
| PL-200 | Retired together with Power Platform Functional Consultant Associate. | 31 August 2026 |
| AZ-500 | Retired together with Azure Security Engineer Associate. | 31 August 2026 |
| AZ-204 | Retired; AI-200 is the closest current path. | 31 July 2026 |
| PL-600 | Retired together with Power Platform Solution Architect Expert. | 30 June 2026 |
| DP-100 | Retired together with Azure Data Scientist Associate; AI-300 is the closest current path. | 1 June 2026 |

Source for each: the exam page on Microsoft Learn linked in the sections below. Retired certifications stay on your transcript with the date you earned them; they simply stop renewing.

## Fundamentals

### AB-900 Copilot and Agent Administration Fundamentals

Microsoft 365 Certified: Copilot and Agent Administration Fundamentals. Beginner level, 45 minutes, English only at launch.

- [Exam page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/exams/ab-900/) - Official.
- [Study guide (skills measured)](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-900) - Official. Data protection and governance 35-40%, Microsoft 365 services and security 30-35%, Copilot and agent administration 25-30%.

## Expert

### MS-102 Microsoft 365 Administrator (retiring)

Microsoft 365 Certified: Administrator Expert. **Retires on 30 November 2026** (11:59 PM Central Standard Time) together with the exam. Requires a prior associate certification (Endpoint Administrator, Teams Administrator, Identity and Access Administrator or Information Security Administrator). Available in Spanish and other languages.

- [Certification page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/m365-administrator-expert/) - Official. Retirement notice and prerequisites.
- [Exam page](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/exams/ms-102/) - Official.
- [Study guide (skills measured)](https://aka.ms/MS102-StudyGuide) - Official. Tenant 10-15%, identity and access 25-30%, security with Defender XDR 35-40%, compliance with Purview 15-20%.
- [Awesome Microsoft 365](https://github.com/fmorrison42/Awesome-Microsoft365) - Community. Admin tools, tips and training links.
- [MS-102 retires on 30 November 2026: what to do](https://www.certifydeck.com/en/articulos/ms-102-se-retira-30-noviembre-2026) - (CertifyDeck) Deadline, prerequisites and alternatives.
- [MS-102 practice test](https://www.certifydeck.com/en/certifications/ms-102) - (CertifyDeck) Practice by domain while the exam is still available.

## Free tools for any exam

- [Microsoft Learn practice assessments](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/practice-assessments-for-microsoft-certifications) - Official. Free, one per exam where available; the best first diagnostic.
- [Microsoft Virtual Training Days](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/trainingdays/) - Official. Free events that often include an exam discount voucher.
- [Microsoft certification voucher offers](https://github.com/nisalgunawardhana/microsoft-certification-voucher-offers) - Community. Tracks current vouchers, beta exams and eligibility rules.
- [Exam Replay](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/certifications/deals) - Official. Paid option that includes a retake.
- [Microsoft Q&A](https://learn.microsoft.com/answers/) - Official forum for technical questions while studying.
- [CertifyDeck](https://www.certifydeck.com) - (CertifyDeck) Practice tests, timed mock exams, study guides and cheat sheets in Spanish and English; you can start for free with no sign-up.
